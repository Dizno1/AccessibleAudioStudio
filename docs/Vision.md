# Vision

AccessibleAudioStudio is not an audio recorder with accessibility added afterward. Accessibility is the architecture.

## The problem

Most audio recording software — browser-based or otherwise — is designed around a waveform. Editing decisions, navigation, and even basic status information are communicated visually: a moving playhead, a colored peak, a shape on a timeline. For a screen reader user, none of that exists. At best it is a wall of silence; at worst it is an interface that announces meaningless pixel coordinates and forces the user to guess.

AccessibleAudioStudio starts from the opposite direction. Every feature is designed first for a keyboard user working entirely through a screen reader, then verified to remain fully usable — and pleasant — for everyone else. Nothing is retrofitted.

## Who this is for

- Blind and low-vision users who need a dependable, professional recording tool that does not assume sight.
- Keyboard-only users who cannot or do not want to rely on a mouse or touch input.
- Sighted users who simply want a fast, uncluttered, distraction-free recording environment.

## What "professional" means here

Professional does not mean visually elaborate. It means:

- Recording reliability a user can trust without watching a meter.
- Control over how the microphone is captured (Recording Profiles), not just a single generic "record" button.
- A library that remembers what was recorded, when, and why — expressed as language, not as icons on a timeline.
- Full functionality from the keyboard alone, including fast shortcuts for the actions used most.

## Long-term direction

Phase 1 builds the dependable recording foundation: device selection, recording, playback, and a library. Later phases (not part of Phase 1) are expected to add editing (trimming, markers), transcription, audio enhancement, and — eventually — integration with VoiceOfOpenDoor. Each of those phases will be built on top of the same screen-reader-first foundation, never around it.

The long-term goal is for AccessibleAudioStudio to be the most efficient browser-based recording environment available for nonvisual users, full stop — not "for an accessible tool."
