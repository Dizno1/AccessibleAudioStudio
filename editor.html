Marks and Selection

Playback

Audition (Space) plays without changing your editing position. Play and Land (X) plays and, when you stop it, moves your editing position to exactly where it stopped. Audible Scrub, Delete, Trim, Select All, and other commands are in the Selection and Playback menus.

Keyboard Shortcuts
  • Tab to the Playhead slider, then Left Arrow / Right Arrow — Move playhead 10 seconds
  • On the Playhead slider, Ctrl+Left Arrow / Ctrl+Right Arrow — Move playhead 30 seconds
  • On the Playhead slider, Home / End — Jump to beginning / end
  • U / I — Scrub 1 second, with audible feedback
  • Shift+U / Shift+I — Scrub 100 milliseconds, with audible feedback
  • Ctrl+Shift+U / Ctrl+Shift+I — Scrub 10 milliseconds, with audible feedback
  • Space — Audition from the current position (does not move it)
  • X — Play and Land: stop moves the playhead to where it stopped
  • [ / ] — Set First Mark / Second Mark at the current position
  • Ctrl+X — Cut
  • Ctrl+C — Copy
  • Ctrl+V — Paste
  • Ctrl+Z — Undo
  • Ctrl+Y — Redo
  • Ctrl+S — Save
  • Ctrl+Shift+S — Save As

The full command set — including Delete Selection, Trim to Selection, Select All, and more — is also in the File, Edit, Selection, Playback, and Navigate menus. Shortcuts do not work while typing in a text field, and every command is reachable without a keyboard shortcut through the menu.
