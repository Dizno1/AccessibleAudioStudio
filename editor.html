Audible Scrub

Listen your way to an exact boundary — the start of a word, a breath, the edge of a sound — a short bit of the audio itself plays at every step, rather than a silent jump.

Playback

Audition (Space) plays without changing your editing position. Play and Land (X) plays and, when you stop it, moves your editing position to exactly where it stopped.

Keyboard Shortcuts
  • Left Arrow / Right Arrow — Move playhead 10 seconds
  • Ctrl+Left Arrow / Ctrl+Right Arrow — Move playhead 30 seconds
  • Home / End — Jump to beginning / end
  • U / I — Scrub 1 second, with audible feedback
  • Shift+U / Shift+I — Scrub 100 milliseconds, with audible feedback
  • Ctrl+Shift+U / Ctrl+Shift+I — Scrub 10 milliseconds, with audible feedback
  • Space — Audition from the current position (does not move it)
  • X — Play and Land: stop moves the playhead to where it stopped
  • [ / ] — Set Selection Start / End at the current position
  • Ctrl+X — Cut
  • Ctrl+C — Copy
  • Ctrl+V — Paste
  • Ctrl+Z — Undo
  • Ctrl+Y — Redo
  • Ctrl+S — Save
  • Ctrl+Shift+S — Save As

Shortcuts do not work while typing in a text field, and every visible button always works as an alternative.
